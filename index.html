Hooks Mobile Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hooks Mobile ("the App") handles personal information when customers use the food-ordering service. Please read it carefully.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, information may include:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Delivery addresses and location information when location access is used.
  • Order details, selected items, delivery instructions and order history.
  • Payment status and transaction-related information needed to process orders.
  • Technical information needed to operate, maintain and secure the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to create and manage accounts, display available restaurants and menus, process orders, arrange deliveries, facilitate payments, communicate about orders, provide customer support, prevent misuse and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

3. Location Information

The App may use delivery addresses and, where permission is granted, device location to support delivery and location-related features. You can manage location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location access may affect features that depend on it.

4. Payments

The App supports online payment and cash on delivery where available. Online payments may be handled by third-party payment providers. Those providers process payment information under their own terms and privacy policies. The information handled by Hooks Mobile depends on the payment integration used.

5. When Information May Be Shared

Information may be shared when necessary to provide the service, including with relevant restaurants, delivery or courier providers, payment processors, technical service providers and other parties involved in fulfilling orders. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or when necessary to protect users and the service.

Information is not described here as being sold or used for advertising purposes; the App operator should update this policy if its actual practices include such activities.

6. Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including fulfilling orders, maintaining required records, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations. Actual retention periods depend on the type of data and applicable requirements.

7. Security

Appropriate technical and organisational safeguards should be used to protect personal information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Account Deletion

You may be able to update certain account information through the App or request assistance from the service operator. To ask about accessing, correcting or deleting your personal information, use the official support contact provided by the Hooks Mobile service operator.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate recordkeeping. The operator should confirm the available account-deletion process and applicable timelines.

9. Children's Privacy

Hooks Mobile is a general food-ordering service and is not specifically directed at children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information should contact the service operator.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the service or its data practices. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests or complaints, contact the Hooks Mobile service operator through its official support channel. Before publication, the operator should provide a working contact method and confirm that the statements in this policy match actual practices.
